GST Registration NOC Guidelines & Checklist

When is a No Objection Certificate (NOC) Required?

Under Section 25 of the CGST Act, 2017 read with Rule 8, you must prove possession of your Principal Place of Business. When the premises is:

  • Owned by parents, spouse, or siblings (Rent-free consent basis).
  • Owned by a Director/Partner who is allowing the Company/LLP to use it.
  • Rented/leased without a registered lease deed, or alongside a rent agreement.
  • Shared office space / Co-working desk.

Documents to Upload Together on the GST Portal:

  1. Duly Signed NOC: The owner must sign at the bottom.
  2. Ownership Proof / Utility Bill: Upload a recent Electricity Bill, Municipal Property Tax Receipt, or Index-II / Registry copy in the owner's name (not older than 60 days).
  3. Owner's Identity Proof: Self-attested copy of the owner's Aadhaar Card or PAN card to establish signature verification.
  4. Proof of Relationship (if family consent): If applying under parental consent, having matching surnames or address on the applicant's Aadhaar helps expedite approval without queries.

Do you need Non-Judicial Stamp Paper?

- For Parental / Family Consent: Plain white A4 paper is accepted in almost all states (e.g., Delhi, Haryana, UP, Karnataka).
- For Commercial / Third-Party Landlord: While plain paper often works, printing on a ₹10, ₹50, or ₹100 non-judicial stamp paper (or notarizing it) is strongly recommended in states like Maharashtra, Gujarat, and Rajasthan to avoid queries under Form GST REG-03.
